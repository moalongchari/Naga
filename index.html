Nagaland's Tribal Foodways

Each of Nagaland’s indigenous tribes preserves specialized fermentation, wood-smoking, and wild herb foraging customs handed down across generations.

Sumi Tribe — Axone Mastery

Known for authentic soybean fermentation wrapped in banana leaves and slow-cooked with cured smoked meats.

Ao Tribe — Anishi Lore

Specialists in pounding and fermenting fresh taro leaves into cakes (Anishi) dried over kitchen hearths.

Angami Tribe — Fresh Herbs & Stews

Famous for rich bamboo shoot stews and wholesome rice porridge dishes cooked with wild forest garlic.
